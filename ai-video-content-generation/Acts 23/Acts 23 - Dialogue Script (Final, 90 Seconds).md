## Scene 1: The Conspiracy Chamber (0:00 – 0:15)

**Visual:**
- **0:00–0:04:** Wide shot of a dimly lit stone chamber packed with 40+ Pharisees huddled tight, whispering, faces lit by oil lamps. Serious and intense.
- **0:04–0:10:** Close-up on the **Pharisee Leader** (holding a scroll), delivering the vow. The others nod gravely.
- **0:10–0:15:** Murmurs of solemn agreement — then a comically loud stomach GRRROWL from the Leader. Everyone freezes. Embarrassed side-glances. The Leader clears his throat.

**Dialogue:**

**PHARISEE LEADER** *(loudly, solemnly)*:
"We make a vow! We shall neither eat nor drink until we have killed Paul!"

*(Solemn murmurs of agreement. Then: GRRROWL — from the Leader's own stomach. Dead silence. He clears his throat and stares straight ahead.)*

## Scene 2: The Time Travelers Arrive (0:15 – 0:35)

**Visual:**
- **0:15–0:20:** An absurdly loud SLURP and CRUNCH cuts through the silence. The Pharisees whip around. Reveal: **Time Traveler #1** and **Time Traveler #2** leaning against the back wall of the chamber in subtly futuristic casual wear — #1 drinking from a glowing juice pipe, #2 holding a crinkling bag of chips.
- **0:20–0:24:** Close on the Pharisee Leader, indignant.
- **0:24–0:35:** The Travelers answer with total nonchalance. The Pharisees exchange confused looks.

**Dialogue:**

**PHARISEE LEADER** *(irritated)*:
"Who are you? You're disrupting our sacred vow!"

**TIME TRAVELER #1** *(finishes a long slurp, unbothered)*:
"Relax, guys! We're here to watch the show."

**TIME TRAVELER #2** *(mouth full, grinning)*:
"You'll see... *CRUNCH.*"

---

## Scene 3: The Commentary (0:35 – 1:00)

**Visual:**
- **0:35–0:45:** **Blocking:** The Time Travelers stand in the foreground facing the camera like pitch-side sports pundits, shoulder to shoulder. Behind them, out of focus and oblivious, the Pharisees argue heatedly over a scroll spread on a table.
- **0:45–0:51:** HARD CUT: the **Roman Commander (Claudius Lysias)** at his desk — Roman columns behind him, sushi plate in front of him. He glances at a delivered report, looks profoundly uninterested, and goes back to his sushi. *(No dialogue — the boredom IS the joke.)*
- **0:51–1:00:** Back to the chamber. The Pharisees behind the Travelers now look visibly hungry and frustrated — one rubs his stomach, another slumps against a pillar.

**Dialogue:**

**TIME TRAVELER #1** *(announcer voice, to camera)*:
"And here's the play of the day — the plot gets filed with the one man legally required to protect Paul. Bold strategy!"

**TIME TRAVELER #2** *(cheerful, popping a chip)*:
"Oh, this is going to be a classic! Meanwhile... where's Paul?"

---

## Scene 4: Paul's Palace Resort (1:00 – 1:12)

**Visual:**
- **1:00–1:05:** Bright, sunlit guest quarters in Herod's Palace opening onto the garden terrace. **Paul**, in clean loose robes, tees up a golf ball on the lawn — broken chains lying discarded on the marble behind him. He swings.
- **1:05–1:08:** The ball rockets across the terrace toward the **Angel**, who is lounging beside the food tray. Without looking up, the Angel snatches the ball out of the air one-handed. Beat.
- **1:08–1:12:** Paul smiles, picks up his fancy glass of juice, and delivers the line — half scripture, half golf trash-talk.

**Dialogue:**

**PAUL** *(cheerfully, as he swings)*:
"Fore!"

*(The Angel catches the ball mid-air without looking. A calm beat.)*

**PAUL** *(smiling, raising his juice)*:
"No weapon formed against me shall prosper."

## Scene 5: The Great Escort (1:12 – 1:22)

**Visual:**
- **1:12–1:22:** Night. A comically massive Roman military escort — hundreds of soldiers, spearmen, and horsemen in torchlight — marches ONE relaxed prisoner out of Jerusalem: Paul, seated comfortably on horseback, giving a small contented wave. In the foreground at the roadside, the **Time Travelers** watch it pass like fans at a parade, still snacking.

**Dialogue:**

**TIME TRAVELER #2** *(excited, gesturing at the column)*:
"They thought they were stopping him... but they just launched him to Rome!"

**TIME TRAVELER #1** *(nodding slowly)*:
"Classic God move — turning traps into triumphs."

## Scene 6: Divine Irony Finale (1:22 – 1:30)

**Visual:**
- **1:22–1:27:** **[EDIT: split-screen composite — two separate generations]** Left side: the conspiracy chamber, all 40 Pharisees clutching their stomachs as a symphony of overlapping growls swells; two of them slowly slide down the wall to the floor. Right side: Paul in the sunny palace, feet up, sipping juice, utterly at peace.
- **1:27–1:30:** Cut to black. Text on screen: ***"The devil is a defeated fool." — Pastor Chris*** over one final, enormous stomach growl.

**Dialogue:** *(None — sound design carries the finale.)*

## Production Notes (Seedance Pipeline)

### Voice direction (goes into each character's @tag line in the prompts)

| Character | Voice |
| --- | --- |
| **Pharisee Leader** | Deep, theatrical, over-solemn — a man performing gravity he can't sustain |
| **Time Traveler #1** | Laid-back, dry, modern casual — the deadpan half of the duo |
| **Time Traveler #2** | Upbeat, energetic, often talking through food — the excitable half |
| **Paul** | Warm, calm, completely unbothered — quiet confidence, never smug |
| **Angel** | No lines in this cut — presence and reflexes only |

Voice consistency across clips: each speaking character should use the same
voice description verbatim in every prompt; if drift is noticeable across
generations, anchor with an @audio reference clip per character.

### Sound cues (in-scene — generate with the clip)

1. Stomach growls — comically loud, the recurring motif (Scenes 1 and 6)
2. Juice pipe slurp — absurdly loud (Scenes 2, 3, 5)
3. Chip crunching — exaggerated (Scenes 2, 3, 5)
4. Golf swing + clean one-handed catch smack (Scene 4)
5. Marching column, hooves, torches crackling (Scene 5)

### Edit-stage notes (post-production, NOT in the Seedance prompts)

- **Music bed:** dark tense strings (Scenes 1–3) → sharp cut to chill lounge (Scene 4) → mock-epic orchestral (Scene 5) → comedic sting + growl symphony (Scene 6). Laid over the full edit for consistency.
- **Scene 6 split screen:** composite of two separately generated clips.
- **Scene 3 hard cut** to the Commander is a straight edit between two clips — no split screen needed.
- **Text card** (1:27–1:30) is added in the editor, not generated.
- Consider layering additional stomach-growl SFX in post so the Scene 1 growl and Scene 6 symphony are audibly the "same" sound.

### Tone

Time Travelers stay light, witty, slightly modern — two friends commenting
on a game they've already seen the ending of. The Pharisees play it 100%
straight; the comedy comes from the contrast, never from them mugging.
