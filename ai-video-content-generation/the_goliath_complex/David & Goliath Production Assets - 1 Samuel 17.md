## 📖 KEY SCRIPTURAL ACCURACY NOTES

### From 1 Samuel 17:4-7 (Goliath's Description)

*   **Height:** 6 cubits and a span (~9 feet 9 inches)
    
*   **Armor weight:** 5,000 shekels of bronze
    
*   **Spear head:** 600 shekels of iron
    
*   **Shield bearer went BEFORE him**
    

### From 1 Samuel 17:40 (David's Preparation)

*   Staff in hand
    
*   5 smooth stones from the **stream**
    
*   Stones placed in **shepherd's bag**
    
*   Sling in hand
    

### From 1 Samuel 17:49-51 (The Victory)

*   Stone struck Goliath's **forehead**
    
*   Goliath fell **face forward** to the ground
    
*   David used **Goliath's own sword** to behead him
