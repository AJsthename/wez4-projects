# The Goliath Complex — Scene & Clip Breakdown

**Project:** The Goliath Complex (ANN — Ancient News Network / David & Goliath, 1 Samuel 17)
**Source script:** [Goliath_Complex_Script.md](Goliath_Complex_Script.md)
**Scale bible:** [crs_table_Goliath_Complex.md](../crs_table_Goliath_Complex.md)
**Purpose:** Divide the full script into **scenes** (by location / dramatic beat) and subdivide each scene into **clips** — the ~8–15 s units that become individual Seedance generations.

---

## How to read this document

- **Scene** = a continuous unit of place + time (one location, one beat of the story). Cuts fall at changes of place or focus.
- **Clip** = one generatable shot or tight run of shots. Target **≤ 15 s** each (Seedance ceiling); most sit at 9–14 s. Dialogue exchanges are kept whole within a clip where the back-and-forth rhythm matters; long monologues get their own clip.
- **Runtime** is an *estimate* for pacing/planning only — final length is set at generation and in the edit.
- **Script lines** link back to the exact beat in the source script.
- Character / prop / location names follow the **Scale Bible** so downstream asset and Seedance prompts stay consistent.

**Totals:** 21 scenes · 95 clips · ~19–20 min estimated master runtime (full episode).

> Naming convention (matches Acts 23): clips are numbered **sequentially across the whole film** (Clip 1 … Clip 95); scenes carry the number + a title. When a scene splits, clips are the atomic unit sent to generation.

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1 — The Two Armies & Goliath's Challenge
**Location:** Valley of Elah — aerial, both mountains, Philistine plain, Israel front, Saul's tent.
**Script:** [L3–L53](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L3-L53) · **Clips 1–7** · ~88s

- **Clip 1 — Aerial establishing → Israel camp** ([L3–L7](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L3-L7))
  Camera soars over mountains (barren, grassy, treed) toward two mountains with a valley between; swerves right, descends to an army encamped with a set of tents on a lower peak. *Subtitles:* "the Valley of Elah" / "Camp of the Army of Israel".
  *Cast:* — (armies as scenery). *Sound:* wind, distant camp murmur, epic drone.
- **Clip 2 — Cross the valley → Philistine army** ([L9](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L9))
  Camera reaches the valley floor and crosses toward the far mountain, revealing it covered by an army that stretches onto the plain.
  *Sound:* rising war-camp ambience.
- **Clip 3 — Shield-bearer + Goliath emerge** ([L11–L13](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L11-L13))
  Camera slows; the **Shield-bearer** steps through shimmering heat onto the plain and stops; **Goliath** emerges behind, dwarfing him, and takes 3 steps forward. Shield-bearer reaches only just above Goliath's waist. *Subtitle:* "GOLIATH OF GATH — Champion of the Philistine Army".
  *Camera (the reveal move — this clip is a single continuous crane):*
  1. **Low angle / bottom-to-top — the false giant.** Camera planted near ground level, tilted up through the heat-shimmer as the Shield-bearer strides out and plants. The worm's-eye angle glorifies him — muscle, bulk, the heavy tower shield read huge; for a beat the audience believes *he* is the champion.
  2. **Continue craning UP — the dwarfing.** Goliath rises into frame behind him; holding the upward move, the camera must keep climbing to contain him, the Shield-bearer's crown sitting only at Goliath's waist. Keep the angle *low* here so Goliath never shrinks — the effort of the tilt-up IS the shock.
  3. **Swing around into a high three-quarter two-shot — the scale gag.** Once we hit Goliath's face, the camera settles at about the height of his helmet and swings a quarter around, looking down the length of both bodies with **both pairs of boots on the same ground line in frame** — the "muscle-man" small beside him but still whole in shot. Goliath then takes his 3 steps forward. *(Revised: this was originally a god's-eye top-down. An overhead throws away the shared ground line the whole comparison rests on, and the model re-guesses both sizes from scratch on the new angle — it was a prime suspect in the runaway-scale failure below.)*
  *Scale lock — the full [scale cage](../crs_table_Goliath_Complex.md#the-scale-cage-bounding-a-giant), not one line:* shield-bearer's crown ≈ just above Goliath's waist **at the belt line**; Goliath's **kneecap ≈ the bearer's hip**; Goliath's hanging **elbow ≈ just above the bearer's crest**; Goliath ≈ 2× the *bearer's* shoulder width; **ceiling** — a little under twice the bearer's height, both men whole in one frame. Restate it in blocking, in the reveal beat, and in the locks.
  ⚠️ **Known failure (3 models, all takes):** with only the single waist landmark, the ratio held ~2s and then Goliath grew into a ~15 m titan whose calves the bearer no longer reached. Cause was unbounded vocabulary (*colossal, monumental scale, earth-shaking, ground tremor*) plus the overhead in step 3 — both now removed. Goliath is a huge **man**; his footfalls are heavier and slower than the bearer's, and the ground stays firm.
  *Props:* Goliath's tower shield, spear ("weaver's beam"), armour.
- **Clip 4 — Taunt #1 + laugh** ([L15–L19](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L15-L19))
  Goliath: *"I see you all cowering on your mountain. Soon to be your grave."* — then he laughs.
- **Clip 5 — The challenge** ([L21–L31](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L21-L31))
  Goliath: *"This is the fortieth day… pick a champion and let him fight me. If he wins…"* (lifts hands) *"we shall be your slaves."* Philistine army roars with laughter; camera catches the **Shield-bearer** snickering.
- **Clip 6 — "If I win, you serve us" + fear** ([L33–L43](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L33-L43))
  Goliath: *"But if I win and kill your champion…"* (lifts hands) *"you shall be our slaves and serve us."* Philistine camp roars in approval; camera pans and zooms to the Israel front line — fear on the soldiers' faces.
- **Clip 7 — Pan up to Saul; "what is your answer?"** ([L45–L53](Goliath_Complex_Script.md#L45-L53))
  Camera pans up to a lower peak: a large tent, soldiers in front, one **head and shoulders above** the rest. *Subtitle:* "KING SAUL — King of Israel". Goliath (VO): *"What is your answer, people of Israel?"*
  *Scale lock:* Saul ~a head above the average soldier.
